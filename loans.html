FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Most lenders require a minimum CIBIL score of 700–750 for home loan approval. A score above 750 typically qualifies you for the best interest rates and higher loan-to-value ratios. We work with lenders who consider applicants with lower scores on a case-by-case basis, particularly when other financial indicators are strong.

Yes. Self-employed individuals, business owners, and professionals can apply using ITR filings, audited financials, and bank statements as income proof. Some lenders also offer low-documentation products for applicants with strong assets and a clean credit history. Our advisors will identify the best program for your profile.

The Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio depends on the loan amount. For loans up to ₹30 lakhs, lenders may finance up to 90% of the property value. For loans between ₹30 lakhs and ₹75 lakhs, the LTV is typically 80%, and for amounts above ₹75 lakhs, it is capped at 75% as per RBI guidelines. Commercial property loans generally have lower LTV ratios of 65–70%.

Yes. Under the Income Tax Act, home loan borrowers can claim deductions of up to ₹2 lakhs per annum on interest paid (Section 24b) and up to ₹1.5 lakhs on principal repayment (Section 80C). First-time homebuyers may also benefit from additional deductions under Section 80EEA, subject to eligibility conditions. Loans Against Property and commercial loans do not qualify for Section 24b benefits.

Common fees include a processing fee (0.25%–1% of loan amount), legal and technical verification charges, stamp duty on loan agreement, and insurance premium for property and life cover. We provide a complete cost breakdown — including all lender fees and third-party charges — before you commit, so there are no surprises at disbursement.
